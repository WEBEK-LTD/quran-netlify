──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Nur Al-Quran — Your Complete Islamic Companion 🌿

A comprehensive, completely free Islamic app. No ads. No subscriptions. No payments. Ever. Created purely for the sake of Allah.

📖 COMPLETE QURAN
• All 114 Surahs in beautiful Ottoman script
• 16 translations in major world languages
• AI-powered Tafsir (interpretation)
• 16 famous reciters: Al-Afasy, As-Sudais, Al-Husary & more
• Browse by Surah or by the 30 Juz

📚 MEMORIZATION SYSTEM
• Track your Hifz surah by surah
• Daily Wird (portion) with streak tracking
• Visual memorization map of all 114 surahs
• Achievement badges for motivation
• Cloud sync across all your devices

🕌 ISLAMIC TOOLS
• Accurate prayer times for any city worldwide + alarm
• Interactive Qibla compass pointing to the Kaaba
• 36 Adhkar & Duas (morning, evening, sleep, prayer)
• Electronic Tasbih counter with goals & session history
• Verse of the Day with tafsir

📻 ISLAMIC RADIO
• Dozens of verified stations: Quran, religious programs, nasheeds
• Powered by Radio Browser global database

📊 STATS & REPORTS
• Monthly reading report with heatmap calendar
• Leaderboard of top readers & memorizers
• Daily, weekly & monthly statistics

🤝 GROUP KHATMA
• Dedicate Quran recitation to souls of loved ones
• Distribute Juz among participants with a share code
• Real-time progress tracking

🔍 MORE FEATURES
• Full-text search across all Quran verses
• Personal notes on any verse
• Share verses as beautiful images
• Works offline
• 6 languages: Arabic, English, Turkish, French, Urdu, Indonesian

Nur Al-Quran is 100% free — for the sake of Allah — with no ads, no subscriptions, and no in-app purchases. 🌿
